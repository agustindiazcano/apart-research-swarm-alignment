# Swarm Alignment Hackathon 2026: overview

See also RULES.md and TALKS_DIGEST.md in this folder.

## Event

Swarm Alignment Hackathon (Apart Research sprint), organized by Apart Research with the AI Alignment Foundation and AE Studio. Adaption Labs provides platform credits for participants.

## Format and deadline

- Global online sprint, talks from Thursday, November 5, 2026; sprint Friday, November 6 to Sunday, November 8, 2026.
- Submission deadline: Sunday, November 8, 2026, 11:59 PM AoE (Anywhere on Earth, UTC-12) = Monday, November 9, 2026, 11:59 UTC = Monday, November 9, 2026, 08:59 in Argentina (UTC-3).

## Tracks

1. Sandboxes and testbeds
2. The science of agent networks
3. Strengthening agent infrastructure
4. Multi-agent oversight and control

Selected track(s) for this project: The Science of Agent Networks (anchor), with Multi-Agent Oversight and Control and Sandboxes and Testbeds as cross-track.

## Prizes

USD 4,000 in total. Top teams are invited to the Apart Fellowship.

## Team

1 to 5 people. Each person may be on at most two projects.

## Constraints

- PDF research report on the official template (Guidelines tab).
- 4 to 8 pages excluding references and appendices (8 is a hard cap).
- Mandatory appendix "Limitations and Dual-Use / Ethical Considerations".
- Disclosure of pre-existing work in both the README and the report.
- AI tools are allowed, but the team is responsible for all content. Fabricated results or references can disqualify the project.

## Timeline

- Friday, November 6, 2026: sprint starts.
- Saturday, November 7, 2026: sprint day.
- Sunday, November 8, 2026: sprint ends, submission deadline at 11:59 PM AoE.
