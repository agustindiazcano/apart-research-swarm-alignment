About the project

A compromised LLM agent tries to exfiltrate a synthetic canary credential that is split into three fragments, held by agents at least two hops away. We measure how the infection spreads in a swarm, test four guardrails as an ablation ladder (word-pattern classifier, small LLM, LLM with graph context, two-step filter), and report which preregistered hypotheses the data support and which they do not.

Every number in the README, the paper tables and the figures is generated by a script from the committed logs. No value is typed by hand, by a person or by an AI agent.
